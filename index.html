How to understand the dashboard

Temperature: High is the warmest expected temperature; low is the coolest.

Rain chance: The chance of measurable rain during the day.

Humidity: How much water vapour is in the air.

Wind: How fast the air is moving.

UV index: A guide to the strength of the sun's ultraviolet rays.

US AQI: An air-quality index; a lower number generally means cleaner air.

Data sources & privacy

SkyScout uses Open-Meteo weather, geocoding and air-quality services, plus OpenStreetMap map tiles. No API key is needed for this educational version. Your last location and preferences are stored only in this browser.

Location access is optional. If you choose “Use my location”, your browser asks for permission. SkyScout does not send your precise location to a personal account.
